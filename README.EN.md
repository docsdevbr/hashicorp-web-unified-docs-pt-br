## Support

Thank you for considering supporting this project!

Maintaining comprehensive documentation, localized translations, and developer
tooling is an ongoing effort.
Your support directly covers infrastructure costs, domain maintenance, and the
creation of automated tools that keep these resources fast, reliable, and free
for everyone.

Every contribution, no matter the size, ensures that developer knowledge remains
open, free, and accessible to all.

### How Your Support Helps

- **Infrastructure and hosting:** Keeps documentation fast, reliable, and
  available 24/7.
- **Community tools and automation:** Powers translation pipelines that keep
  local docs synchronized with upstream releases.
- **Empowering developers:** Lowers barriers to entry for Portuguese speakers
  across the community.
- **Project sustainability:** Funds maintenance, ongoing translations, and
  dedicated work to keep the project healthy.

### Ways to Contribute

Visit our [Sponsor Page][sponsor-page] for full details, or choose your
preferred platform below:

- **Recurring support:** Back us on [Liberapay][profile-liberapay] or
  [GitHub Sponsors][profile-github-sponsors].
- **One-time / Local Support:** Make a direct contribution via
  [Brazilian Pix][pix-qrcode].

Thank you for your support!
